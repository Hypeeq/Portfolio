Abstract/Introduction

In my second co-op term as a Health Informatics Specialist at the Ontario Veterinary College (OVC), University of Guelph, I expanded my technical expertise and professional skills in a hospital environment. I worked on system maintenance, troubleshooting, workflow optimization, and automation using Microsoft Power Automate, while also strengthening teamwork, communication, and time management. This experience confirmed my passion for using IT to enhance healthcare operations.

Job Description

During this work term, my role revolved around maintaining and optimizing healthcare IT systems:

  • System Maintenance: I ensured the upkeep and smooth operation of critical medical systems, including troubleshooting and resolving hardware and software issues.
  • System Integration: I assisted in the integration of new systems into the existing healthcare infrastructure, ensuring seamless functionality across platforms.
  • Ticket Management: I managed and resolved technical issues through the ticketing system, providing timely solutions for IT support requests.
  • Access Control: I managed user access rights and permissions, ensuring secure and appropriate access to medical systems and patient data.
  • Documentation: I maintained comprehensive system documentation and manuals to ensure that technical processes and system integrations were well-documented for future reference and troubleshooting.

The skills I utilized included troubleshooting, system integration, access control management, and clear communication. While my coursework provided a foundation in general IT and programming, I significantly expanded my practical experience in maintaining complex healthcare systems and collaborating with multidisciplinary teams.

Conclusions

This work term at the Ontario Veterinary College was an important step in my professional development, giving me the chance to apply problem-solving and critical thinking skills to real-world challenges. One example was addressing inconsistent data entry in the patient management system, where I worked directly with users to identify the issue, propose a clearer input format, and create supporting documentation. The improvement not only reduced confusion but also reinforced the importance of creative, user-focused problem solving in healthcare IT.

Collaboration and communication were also central to my experience. By working closely with the Health Informatics team and hospital staff, I learned how to adapt my communication style depending on the audience, whether it was explaining technical processes to non-technical staff or providing detailed updates to colleagues. These experiences enhanced my integrative communication skills and taught me how teamwork contributes to better results, especially in a fast-paced environment where reliability and accuracy are critical.

On the technical side, I expanded my literacy with healthcare systems such as Imprivata, ezyVet, and Footprints, while also exploring automation tools like Microsoft Power Automate to streamline workflows. Balancing these technical tasks with ongoing ticket management required strong time management, and I became more effective at organizing priorities to meet both immediate and long-term needs. Overall, this term allowed me to strengthen all five of my learning goals—problem solving, teamwork, technological literacy, communication, and time management—while deepening my passion for using technology to improve healthcare operations.

Reflection

This term at OVC allowed me to apply my technical skills directly to healthcare IT challenges. I gained hands-on experience with systems like Imprivata, ezyVet, and Footprints while also exploring automation through Microsoft Power Automate. These tasks strengthened both my problem-solving abilities and technological literacy.

I also grew in teamwork and communication by working with staff across departments. Explaining technical issues in simple terms and collaborating on solutions taught me how important clear communication is in a hospital environment.

Time management was another key area of growth. Balancing support tickets, documentation, and integration work helped me build stronger organizational habits. Overall, this term improved my technical, professional, and interpersonal skills, giving me confidence for future roles.
